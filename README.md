# Birthday Music Finder 🎶

Discover which Spotify songs were released on your birthday!  

---

## Features

- Enter your birthday (day, month, year).  
- Log in with Spotify to access your liked tracks and playlists.  
- Finds songs released:  
  - **Full Match**: day, month, and year match.  
  - **Partial Match**: only day and month match.  
- Progress bar shows playlist loading.  
- Clickable links to Spotify tracks.  

---

## Usage

1. Open [main page](https://gevorg-tsat.github.io/your-birthday-songs/) in a browser.  
2. Select your birthday and click **Continue**.  
3. Log in to Spotify.  
4. View songs matching your birthday.
